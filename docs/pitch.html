The product

Five questions in, a legal disclosure out.

No lawyer. No developer. The seller answers in their own language — the disclosure is always generated in Korean, because that is who has to be able to read it.

1. 사용후기 작성 권한: 실제 구매(결제)가 확인된 고객에게 작성 권한이 있습니다.

Then one line

Paste this anywhere. It appears.

<script src="https://trustlabel.store/api/embed/abc123" async></script>

Served cross-origin with stale-while-revalidate, so a CDN keeps showing the last good copy even if we go down. Every failure path returns valid JavaScript — our outage must never break a seller's page.

Cafe24 and Imweb support this. Naver Smart Store blocks external scripts outright, so those sellers get the copy-paste block instead — knowing which is which is half the product.

And then we check

Is it actually showing?

Paste your storefront URL. We fetch the page and look for it. Three answers, never two:

  1. FoundThe disclosure is live on your page.
  2. Not foundWe read your page and it is not there.
  3. Could not checkWe could not reach the page. That is our problem, not yours — and collapsing it into “not found” would be a false accusation.
